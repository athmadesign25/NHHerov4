Basic Health Checkup

Essential screenings for a healthy lifestyle, ideal for individuals under 30.

  • Complete Blood Count (CBC)
  • Lipid Profile (Cholesterol)
  • Liver Function Test
  • Physician Consultation

Master Health Check

Advanced diagnostic profile with cardiac and specialist consults. Ideal for 30-50 yrs.

  • Cardiac Risk Markers (ECG, TMT)
  • Kidney & Liver Function
  • Thyroid Profile
  • Cardiologist Consultation
  • Dietary Counseling

Senior Citizen Wellness

Specialized screenings tailored for age-related health monitoring (50+ yrs).

  • Bone Mineral Density
  • Prostate/Breast Screening
  • Vitamin D & B12 Levels
  • Geriatric Consultation
